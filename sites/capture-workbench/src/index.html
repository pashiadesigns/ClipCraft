A few useful details.

Can it take screenshots yet?

Yes. Choose Capture or press Ctrl+Alt+C from another app while ClipCraft is running, including minimized. Drag a region; Escape or right-click cancels. Help shows shortcut status and a session toggle. Custom keys, scrolling capture and mixed-DPI qualification remain on the roadmap.

Where are my images stored?

ClipCraft automatically saves editable images and text drafts in your Windows local application data folder: ClipCraft/history-v1. Wait for Saved to Library, then close and reopen from the Library. The current limit is 50 images / 512 MB; a full Library preserves older work and asks you to save a separate project. You choose where to save project copies and exported images. Older app versions cannot read the updated Library index.

What is inside the download?

A portable Windows x64 desktop application, its command-line companion, an example project, and a getting-started file. Extract the archive before opening the application.

Is this affiliated with Snagit or ArtCraft?

No. ClipCraft is an independent product under development, with original branding and selectively adapted open-source components.
